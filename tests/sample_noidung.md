## Small Language Models

Small Language Models (SLMs) are efficient alternatives to large models. Microsoft Phi-4-mini with 4 billion params achieves comparable results to much larger models. Google Gemma 3 requires only 529MB RAM for its 1B parameter version [13]. SLMs enable on-device AI with privacy and low latency benefits.

## RAG + Knowledge Management

RAG combines retrieval with generation for accurate responses. The pipeline has three stages: retrieval, context preparation, and generation. Hybrid retrieval uses BM25 keyword matching plus vector search [3]. On-device RAG enables private AI without cloud dependency.

## Responsible AI

Responsible AI has become a regulatory requirement globally. The EU AI Act classifies AI applications into four risk levels. OECD provides six-step due diligence guidance [11]. Only 25% of companies have mature RAI frameworks according to BCG/MIT research [12].
